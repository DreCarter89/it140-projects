## Theme and Storyline

**Theme:**

Galactic Survival Mission

**Storyline:**

The player takes on a role as a space ranger who awakens aboard a space station after a mysterious system failure. The station has lost power, communication with Earth has been disconnected, and a dangerous alien is roaming the facility. In order to survive, the space ranger must explore eight different rooms and collect six essential items needed to power up the station's emergency escape system. The player must carefully navigate the station while avoiding the alien. If the space ranger encounters the dangerous alien before collecting all six items, the mission fails. Once all six items are collected, the space ranger can enter the alien domain, activate the emergency escape system, and successfully complete the mission. 

## Rooms

Project One requires a minimum of eight rooms.

1. Command Center (Start room)
2. Engineering room
3. Power Room
4. Communications Room
5. Storage Room
6. Science lab
7. Medical Room
8. Gorgan's Domain (Villain room)

Add more rooms if your design needs them.

## Items

With the minimum eight-room design, Project One requires at least six items.
Every room except the start room and villain room must contain one item.

1. Access Card
2. Oxygen Tank
3. Battery Cell
4. Repair Tool Kit
5. Medical Kit
6. Radio Antenna 

If you add rooms beyond the minimum, add an item for every additional room
except the start room and villain room.

## Villain

The villain's name is Gorgan, a dangerous alien creature that has taken control of the space station. Gorgan has dark yellow eyes, sharp teeth and powerful claws. He is highly intelligent, aggressive, and determined to prevent the space ranger from escaping. The player must avoid Gorgan until the essential items have been collected.
